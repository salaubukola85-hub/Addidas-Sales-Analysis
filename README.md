# Adidas Sales Analysis (2020–2021)

## Project Overview
This project explores Adidas sales data from 2020 to 2021 using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to analyse sales performance, identify trends, understand product and regional performance, and generate insights that can support business decisions.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and retailers.
- Analyse sales trends over time.
- Compare sales performance across regions and states.
- Understand the contribution of different sales methods.
- Evaluate operating profit and operating margin.

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains Adidas sales records from 2020 to 2021.

Key columns include:
- Retailer
- Invoice Date
- Region
- State
- City
- Product
- Price per Unit
- Units Sold
- Total Sales
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin
- Sales Method

## Data Cleaning and Preparation
The following activities were performed:
- Checked for missing values.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Reviewed data types and formatting.
- Standardised relevant text fields.
- Validated sales calculations.
- Reviewed operating profit and operating margin values.

## Business Questions
1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. Which region generated the highest sales?
3. Who are the top 10 retailers by sales?
4. Which products generated the highest revenue?
5. How did sales change over time?
6. Which sales method contributed the most revenue?
7. Which products recorded the highest units sold?
8. Which region generated the highest operating profit?
9. Which products had the highest operating margin?
10. Which retailers contributed the most to total sales?
11. What percentage of total sales came from each region?
12. What trends can be identified from the 2020–2021 sales performance?
